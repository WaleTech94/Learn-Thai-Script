# v7.2 Economy Notes

Prepared: 2026-07-04

## Earn Table

- Daily route complete: +10 tokens.
- Spell it first-time correct item: +2.
- Class sprint at 85% or higher: +3.
- Reading-room story first read: +3.
- Optional phrase rehearsal from the weekly five: +5.
- Mastery checkpoint first pass: +15.
- Tone practice badge: +30 first earn, +10 renewals.
- New personal-best flashcard lap: +3.

## Weekly Personas

Mid-course learner: the normal daily route gives 7 x +10 = 70 tokens. A focused week with a couple of optional extras, such as one class sprint and one story first read, lands around 76 tokens. Rehearsing one weekly phrase instead lands around 75 tokens. This supports a 60-token phrase pack in about one focused week without changing routine earn rates.

Completed maintenance learner: the maintenance Today route still uses the same completion reward once review, one maintenance main task and the practice/lighter-day requirement are clear, so the weekly base is also 70 tokens. Optional re-reading, rematches and Wild deck keep the learner active without adding new paid gates. A normal maintenance week therefore also lands around 70-80 tokens.

## Price Ladder

- Titles: 25.
- Streak freeze: 30.
- Themes: 40-60.
- Ranat sound pack: 50.
- Phrase packs: 50-60, with the new Taxi & Grab and Market bargaining packs at 60.
- Bangkok reads story pack: 80.

Existing earn rates were left unchanged. The v7.2 pricing uses new-item prices to absorb the added shop surface rather than punishing the routine.

## v8.1 Street Arcade additions

- Parcel Sort: free milestone unlock after Lesson 2.
- Night Market Hunt: 50 tokens after Lesson 4.
- Tuk-Tuk Tone Run: 60 tokens after Lesson 13.
- Ekkamai Sunset: 40 tokens.
- Yaowarat Neon: 50 tokens.
- Tuk-Tuk Chrome: 60 tokens.

Cabinets do not pay repeatable tokens, so they cannot become a farming loop. Their underlying learning equivalents stay free; the purchase buys the optional game presentation. At the established 70-80 token normal week, a learner can generally choose one new cabinet or one theme per focused week without changing earn rates.
